## Art & images
- The rune stone (hero), its slate textures (albedo, bump and roughness maps, generated on a canvas at runtime since v2.2), the rune glyphs and the favicon are procedural or hand-written code in this repo (`lib/runes.ts`, `lib/stone.ts`, `components/hero/*`, `app/icon.svg`). The SVG stone is generated from the 3D geometry by `scripts/stone-profile.mjs`. They use no third-party assets.
- `public/stone/face-740.avif` and `face-1480.avif` (v2.5, the SVG stone's desktop face) are renders of that same procedural 3D stone, baked by `scripts/bake-stone.mjs` (no runes, no fire light). No third-party asset.
- `public/textures/grain.png` (page and slab grain) is procedurally generated by `scripts/make-grain.mjs`. No third-party asset.
- Everything under `public/images/` is Lars Crawford's own work, carried over from the original site: artwork, project screenshots, research micrographs and photos.
- `public/images/science/plates/` (v2.3) are square crops of four of those research images, made by `scripts/crop-science.mjs` to remove instrument info bars, a time header and Blender viewport axes. Same author and terms as the originals, which stay unchanged beside them.
- No third-party art assets are used. Any CC0 asset added later must be listed here with its source URL.
